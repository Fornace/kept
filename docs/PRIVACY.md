# Privacy

Kept does not collect anything.

- Receipt photos, extracted fields and notes are stored in the app's own container on your device.
- The only data shared with another process is the deadline summary written to the App Group file
  that the widget reads. Both live on the same device.
- Receipts are read with Apple's Vision framework and, when the device has Apple Intelligence, with
  Apple's on-device Foundation Models. No receipt leaves the device and no network request carries it.
- There is no account, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no advertising SDK.

Deleting a receipt deletes its photo and its deadlines. Settings ▸ Delete every receipt removes the
whole store.
