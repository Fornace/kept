## Description

The receipt goes in a drawer. The deadline goes with it.

Kept keeps both. Photograph the paper, or hand it a PDF from your inbox, and Kept reads the shop, the date and the total on the device itself. Then it watches the two clocks that matter:

- Return windows, counted down to the last day you can take something back.
- Warranties, so a fault is reported while the shop still pays for the repair.

Nothing is guessed silently. Every scan opens a check sheet with the fields Kept read, so a wrong total is one tap away from right. When the receipt states its own terms, Kept uses them; when it does not, Kept suggests what shops in that category usually offer and says so.

WHAT IT DOES

- Reads photographed, shared and PDF receipts with Apple's Vision and, where available, Apple Intelligence, entirely on your device.
- Tracks return windows and warranty endings side by side, with a countdown you can read at a glance.
- Warns you before a deadline, three days ahead for returns and thirty for warranties, at a time of day you pick.
- Shows what is still returnable and what is still covered, in money, on one screen.
- Keeps line items, serial numbers and notes with the photo.
- Adds a widget with your nearest deadlines, and works with Siri and Shortcuts: "What can I still return in Kept?"
- Syncs nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics.

WHO IT IS FOR

- Anyone who buys something they might take back.
- Anyone who has ever paid for a repair that was still under warranty.
- Anyone whose receipts live in a shoebox, a photo roll and three email accounts.

## App Review notes

Kept has no account and no server. Everything, including receipt photos and every extracted field,
is stored in the app's own container on the device. The only shared storage is an App Group file
holding the deadline summary that the widget renders.

To see the app with data, add a receipt from the + button: choose a photo, a PDF or "Type it in".
Apple Intelligence is optional; when the system model is unavailable Kept reads the receipt with
Vision and marks suggested windows as suggestions in the check sheet.
